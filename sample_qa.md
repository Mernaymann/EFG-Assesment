\# Sample Q\&A



\## 1. How many annual leave days do employees get?



\*\*Answer:\*\*  

Employees are entitled to 21 working days of annual leave.



\*\*Source:\*\* Annual Leave Policy — Entitlement



\---



\## 2. Can employees carry over unused annual leave?



\*\*Answer:\*\*  

Unused annual leave may be carried over according to the conditions specified in the Annual Leave Policy.



\*\*Source:\*\* Annual Leave Policy — Carryover



\---



\## 3. How much paid sick leave are employees entitled to?



\*\*Answer:\*\*  

Employees are entitled to up to 14 working days of paid sick leave per calendar year.



\*\*Source:\*\* Sick Leave and Medical Absence Policy — Entitlement



\---



\## 4. Can employees work remotely?



\*\*Answer:\*\*  

Remote work is permitted according to the eligibility requirements and conditions specified in the Remote Work Policy.



\*\*Source:\*\* Remote Work Policy — Eligibility
